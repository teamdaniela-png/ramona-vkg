# Rule learning over the documented offers: first results

Exploratory. Generated by `scripts/rule_learning/learn_rules.py` (standard library, seed 2026). Re-run the script to regenerate this page and the tables in `data/processed/rule_learning/`.

## Question

Which combinations of signals separate an offer documented as a trap from a posting of a verified employer, and do they agree with the hand-written red flags of RATR-O (Type A, classic; Type B, credible)?

## Examples

- Documented offers: 295 press-documented offers with audit status *verificada*; 167 of them in Mexico, of which 93 are criminal recruitment.
- Verified postings: 37 postings of verified employers, all in Mexico (of 51 captured).
- 32 binary features: the 16 hand-written flags, job family, pay, requirements, channel and target (`data/processed/rule_learning/examples.csv`).
- Weekly pay in MXN is known for 32 documented offers (median 4,000) and 20 verified postings (median 2,458, maximum 6,236). Monthly amounts are divided by 4.33.

## Method

- Exhaustive search of conjunctions of up to three conditions, minimum 5 offers covered. One-sided Fisher exact test per rule, Benjamini-Hochberg correction over all rules tested in the task; a rule is reported when q < 0.05 and no shorter rule over a subset of its conditions is at least as confident.
- Greedy sequential-covering rule list (Laplace precision, at most three conditions, precision of at least 80% on the training fold).
- Decision tree of depth 3 (Gini) and majority class as baselines.
- 5-fold stratified cross-validation repeated 20 times; mean ± standard deviation over repetitions.

## Limits

- The two sides of Task 1 were not observed the same way. A verified posting is read in full. A documented offer is known through what a journalist reported, so a missing flag means *not reported*, not *absent*. Flag rates on the documented side are lower bounds, and any rule built on the absence of a signal is unsafe.
- The verified postings come from formal job boards and four job categories. Informal, word-of-mouth recruitment is not represented. Channel and target features are excluded from Task 1 for that reason.
- 37 verified postings is a small negative class. Confidence values near 100% mean *no verified posting in this sample*, not *no legitimate posting*.
- In Task 2, `pay_stated` means that the source reports an amount, not that the other offers had none.
- Press coverage over-represents heavily reported cases; deduplication is conservative.
- The rules are hypotheses about documented cases. They are not prevalence estimates and have not been tested on offers submitted by users.
